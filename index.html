Our Philosophy

Where fire meets finesse.

NOIR was built on a simple idea: cook fewer dishes, cook them with total attention, and let the fire do most of the talking. Our kitchen works almost entirely over live coals — a discipline that rewards patience and punishes shortcuts. The room stays low-lit and quiet, so the only thing competing for your attention is what's on the plate.
